# Season 1 mainland and coast design

Build and review one connected inland–ocean route before applying the landscape style to all 16 territories. The first route uses an existing road through Pine Reach and the north bridge to reach R7's actual river mouth. A proposed R9 estuary links that mouth to the west lighthouse. The first art review can stop at the estuary. The offshore leg can join when the ocean is built.

R7 surface with all 16 proposed mainland treatments and the north estuary test route — see `mainland-coast.png` in the review archive or regenerate locally.

**Pass the first joint art review when** a player can follow the road without coordinates, see where to leave the pickup, board and return without moving any blocks, and tell from boat height where the trees thin into an estuary. Use the full existing route width wherever it is wider.

## The rest of the mainland

City names, resources and income remain the baseline for this study. The outer two rows each have two starter cities, so a solo player can still found in A, D, M or P and choose the direction of travel.

Before authoring, exclude existing roads, bridges, river widths and banks, registered city siege/restoration boxes, every plot and all-tier factory / hall footprints, rear resource routes, wall guards and gate approaches. Protect the full central court (X/Z −150 through +150 inclusive) and all monument footprints. Keep the historical changed-block and protected-column maps from R8-B. Require zero writes in the corresponding current protection masks. Outside the masks, use irregular patches that thin around roads and city entries, then recheck sightlines and turning room on foot and with a pickup. For the two shoreline joins, also check for isolated pools, visible generator seams, and sustained chunk loads in both travel directions.

All work here is a design study. No area of an existing development save or the candidate kit was modified. Site reservation, sea lanes and the pilot berth are proposed. Seabed, water depth after integration, bank transfer and boat movement still require a disposable, integrated world. Raw survey centerlines, in block coordinates, and the proposed treatments are in [mainland-coast.json](mainland-coast.json).
