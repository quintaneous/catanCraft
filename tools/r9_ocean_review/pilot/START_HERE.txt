CatanCraft | Pine Reach to Brinewatch
A Creative terrain review for Minecraft Java Edition 1.20.1

OPEN THE WORLD ON WINDOWS
1. In File Explorer, right-click the downloaded ZIP and choose Extract All.
2. Press Windows + R, enter %APPDATA%\.minecraft\saves and press Enter.
   If your launcher uses a separate game directory, open its saves folder instead.
3. Copy the CatanCraft_Pine_Reach_Brinewatch folder directly into saves. Open
   that folder once and check that level.dat and region are directly inside it.
4. Open vanilla Minecraft Java Edition 1.20.1, choose Singleplayer, and open
   the Pine Reach / Brinewatch review world. Its starting point is the road in
   Pine Reach. You can walk, fly, or take a boat from the Creative inventory.

TAKE THE TRIP
Head east from spawn along the broad gravel road. The new spruce and birch
groves are on either side. Where the road approaches the north bridge, leave
the road on your left just before the stone deck. Follow the sloping gravel
and sand down to the water. Place a boat just off the bank and right-click
to board. Face north and follow the bends to the sea.

At the mouth, follow the lit green-and-white marks, keeping them to your
right on the way out. Taller paired lights mark the turns. Turn west along
the northern coast, south at the northwest corner, then west to the lighthouse.
Approach its main landing from the east. Left Shift dismounts. The second
landing is on the north side. On the way home, keep the markers on your left.

OPTIONAL SHORTCUTS
Creative mode includes flight. If you prefer to start partway through the
trip, these chat commands use coordinates in this review world.

Bridge approach, looking east      /tp @s -44.5 81 -567.5 -90 0
Launch bank, looking north         /tp @s -25.5 76 -569.5 180 0
Northern mouth, above the river    /tp @s 43.5 77 -1023.5 180 0
Lighthouse main landing            /tp @s -1384.5 77 -430.5 90 0

This is a standalone landscape study. Its offshore build covers the surveyed
northern and western route. CatanCraft progression, nation founding and
monument rewards belong to the separate Forge gameplay kit. Boat handling,
the view from each bend and how easily you can find the landing are the
main questions for a first-person visit.
