# Gathering and deliveries in the terrain release

Proposed first playtest: find a wood node in Pine Reach, harvest a load, take it down to the north bridge, and deliver it by boat to Brinewatch. Offer a shorter inland contract that a new player can complete on foot. Someone who has earned a pickup can use it on the road leg and compare the time saved. Leave both trips optional and keep ordinary deliveries available between monument events.

| Physical feature | What the player sees or does | What the joint playtest decides |
|---|---|---|
| Gathering clearings | Scattered, handpicked sites in terrain that suits the resource. A fallen log pile can represent wood, an exposed seam stone, and a produce collection site agriculture. The server chooses among eligible anchors in each region's specialty. Anyone can gather there, including visitors from another nation. | Can players find a first node from a road and a better one by exploring? Use an interaction or timed harvest against the registered node. Leave terrain blocks protected. Compare finding, gathering and hauling time with the reward. |
| Freight exchanges | Reuse logistics centers and existing docks. A labeled dispatch board offers a nearby run and a longer haul with destination, cargo and payment in one view. A marked unloading spot shows where to finish. | Can a new player accept, find the destination and unload without entering names or coordinates? Reserve a shipment once and settle it once, including restart, disconnect and two players reaching the same delivery point. |
| Road-to-water transfers | Give boats room to approach the bank and pickups room to turn clear of through traffic. Use existing bridge and shore geometry where possible. Add a small turnout or landing only where the vehicle test shows a need. | Test a loaded road approach, unloading on foot, launching, landing and returning with the same pickup still accessible. The first bank at B1 already has a surveyed route. |
| Wayfinding | Use a destination sign at a decision point, a territory name at the crossing and visible lights at sea turns. Use the same destination names on signs and the contract board. | Can a first-time player reach the correct dock at a normal view distance without coordinates? Can they tell when they enter another nation's territory before deciding to gather there? |

For agriculture, try produce deliveries from an agricultural region to an inland depot or a lighthouse provisioning point. This gives farms a repeat destination and makes a viable contract for a player on foot. For the coastal trip, use ordinary freight so a player can dock and get paid while the monument is dormant. Reserve the rare prize for the monument event itself.

## What to measure on the first trip

- Time spent finding a node, harvesting, loading, travelling and unloading. Record net resources and cash per active minute alongside passive income over the same interval.
- Cargo per inventory slot and trips required on foot, by pickup and by boat. Trial stack sizes and harvest times together. Preserve a worthwhile return for a player who chooses to grind.
- Two players reaching the same node, harvesting across a territory boundary, interrupted deliveries, a full inventory and a blocked unloading spot. Verify one harvest per depletion and one payout per accepted delivery.
- Check all 16 territories against their specialty, roads, building and wall reservations, and the neutral monument areas. Test access from each founding city. Compare server tick times, memory and chunk loads on the same route with and without the added interactions.

The creative pilot currently contains Pine Reach, the original B1 transfer, the estuary, lit sea markers and Brinewatch. Node interactions, contract boards, physical cargo, rewards and the suggested roadside signs are proposals for the terrain release. The price table, stack limits and respawn intervals can be set after timed trips.
