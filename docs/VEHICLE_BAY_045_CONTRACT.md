# ECO-03 — bounded fixed-bay policy, 045

`VehicleBay45` is an **unregistered policy backend**. No actual factory counter, native delivery bay, purchase route, debit, spawn, refund or recovery journal is activated. Native geometry/access qualification and a durable entity delivery adapter remain required before player-facing vehicle purchases can use it. Existing vehicle purchases and their prices are unchanged.

A facility identity binds city, actual industrial plot, nation, dimension, source revision/hash, resolved anchor/rotation, counter and completed level. A bay binds one vehicle identifier and pinned native geometry hash to that exact facility, a finite fractional spawn position, cardinal heading and immutable local support/clearance/boarding/departure masks. Geometry hashes are only identity: a real native probe must independently certify initialized body/turret/door/passenger geometry and departure sweep for the declared orientation. Synthetic tests do not provide that certification.

Local cell coordinates are block offsets from the resolved facility anchor. Supported transforms are the existing factory rotations 0/180. Fractional positions rotate around the anchor block center, matching the discrete schematic cell transform; no rounding to block origins occurs. The complete native vehicle envelope, not just its spawn cell or registered AABB, must be represented by the certified clearance mask. Every mask cell must be loaded and owned; every clearance cell must be clear of blocks, fluids and entities, and every support cell must provide full support. The backend does not force-load chunks, clear obstructions, move entities or search near the player.

The explicit registry is bounded to16 bays, rejects duplicate IDs/foreign facilities, and uses stable registration order. Opening a quote may choose another explicitly registered compatible clear bay. Confirmation is bound to the original bay; obstruction does not silently relocate the vehicle. A changed registry entry, source identity, owner, actor, level, dimension or factory orientation invalidates the quote. Presence checks require current authenticated living leader, counter reach/ray, completed source and no pending construction/siege. These booleans are an adapter contract, not a substitute for server-native observation.

The provisional1200-tick session lifetime follows the existing weapon shop policy; it is not an activated vehicle UX promise. Confirmation rejects exact expiry and clock rollback. The immutable existing `ShopPurchase40.Price` record preserves quoted prices; this backend does not consult new simulation proposals, debit funds or reuse player-inventory delivery as vehicle durability. A later vehicle journal must bind the exact native entity UUID/type/nation, fixed bay and original cost, and reconcile durable entity presence before replay.

Eight `catancraft_vehicle45` GameTests cover fractional/discrete transforms, missing/foreign/duplicate registries, every clearance/support/ownership/native-geometry refusal, deterministic alternative selection versus no confirmation relocation, physical authority/pending work, stale/foreign/expired quotes, immutable masks/prices, and invalid/unbounded masks/positions. They use only synthetic in-memory policy fixtures, without world writes. Consult the root validation checkpoint for actual build/native execution results. This implementation alone does not close SEC-01 or approve actual installed-pack behavior.


All eight fixed-bay policy GameTests passed in run43112426119. This validates the unregistered policy and synthetic probes, not native vehicle geometry or public purchasing. See [exact evidence](ECONOMY_VEHICLE_045_CHECKPOINT.md).
