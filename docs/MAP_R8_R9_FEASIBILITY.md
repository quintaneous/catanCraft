## Coast, boats and the world border

The clean R7 save records a stationary 2,000-block-square border centered at X/Z 0, and its 16,384 authored chunks cover X/Z −1,024 through 1,023. The brief proposes **350–600 blocks of navigable water beyond the new coastline in most directions**. Preserving the mainland at its present scale requires extending the authored world. Moving the border alone would expose the save's plains-flat generator. Scanning the current mainland perimeter found river water at the north edge at X 27 through 48 and the south edge at X −46 through −28. Both side edges are grass-topped. Use the existing river exits when designing estuaries. If the coast stayed near ±1,000, a square extension of 350–600 blocks would require about 12,516–23,616 additional chunks before render margins. Coastline and border dimensions are still design decisions.

After full R8 delivery and approval, review a beach or cove alongside a second example featuring a cliff, headland or estuary. A global plan should show river mouths and bridges, a continuous deep-water route around the mainland, the measured distance to the actual square border, and safe approaches through shallow water. Reserve **three to five possible offshore sites**. Record an anchor, footprint, depth, approach channel and distances to shore and border for each. Keep shipping lanes clear. The owner wants additional monuments at sea. Their locations, structures, capture rules and rewards are still open. Boat access is part of the terrain brief. Naval combat is a separate design decision.
