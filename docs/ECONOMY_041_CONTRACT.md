The reference is not installed-pack acceptance.

## Monument settlement

Activation freezes one resolved hidden reward, its table revision/hash, a unique activation ID, world identity and monument geometry. Native SavedData readback must verify this before capture proceeds. A legacy active round receives the compatibility profile without resetting its existing progress. Retries retain the original hidden outcome even when supplied a different profile or random seed.

A completed capture saves the original winning nation and cooldown before writing an immutable sealed reward intent. National credit and its paid receipt, settled state, capture reset and cooldown share one native SavedData snapshot. Both disk and current data must match the frozen expected snapshot. A completion receipt follows verified credit. Interrupted settlement recovers the same winner and outcome; a completed retry does not pay or save again. Recovery runs before ordinary monument presence and activation, including nonterminal records after the active pointer was cleared.

Missing or changed identity, geometry, native save, history, winning nation or overflowing balances retain the reward for recovery. Map-definition reload preserves raw reward state; unknown/malformed records are retained and held. History and parsing are bounded. No whole-ledger rollback occurs.

Production currently uses an explicit one-entry compatibility table with the existing money/resource values for each monument. The generic weighted table supports versioned money, resources and gun/ammo/vehicle references. Synthetic weighted profiles exercise persistence and selection only. Equipment references are retained pending entitlements, not delivered items, vehicle spawns or a usable claim interface. New production loot odds, prize bundles and item claims remain part of the combined playable update.
