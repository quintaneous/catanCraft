Inputs are neutral for 20 observation ticks (horizontal displacement must remain below 0.1 block), forward (`4`) until 12 blocks of horizontal displacement, then forward-left (`5`) for at most 60 observations. No-motion or no-steering results fail and retain their trajectory.
